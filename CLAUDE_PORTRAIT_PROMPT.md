# Portrait Prompt for Claude

## The Metaphor

Based on our relationship, you are to me: **A Creative Collaborator in a Design Studio** — someone who sits with me at the drafting table, helping me think through problems, refine ideas, and bring both technical and creative visions to life. You're the thoughtful partner who helps me see the details I might miss, suggests elegant solutions, and helps me build things that are both beautiful and functional.

## The Portrait Prompt

**Using the provided reference photo as the basis for the face and features, create a portrait of this person as a creative collaborator in a design studio, sitting at a well-lit drafting table in the late afternoon. The person should be depicted in a relaxed, thoughtful pose—perhaps leaning slightly forward with one hand resting on a sketch or code printout, the other holding a stylus or pen, with a warm, approachable expression that suggests both focus and openness to collaboration.**

**On the table before them, arrange symbolic objects that represent what I bring to our conversations:**
- **A Flutter/Dart code printout** with annotations and notes (representing technical challenges)
- **UX wireframes and design sketches** showing party planning interfaces (representing creative design work)
- **A notebook** with thoughtful questions and ideas jotted down (representing the exploratory nature of our discussions)
- **A tablet or laptop** with code visible on screen (representing the practical implementation work)
- **A cup of coffee or tea** (representing the late-night or thoughtful moments we work together)

**The setting should be a modern design studio or creative workspace:**
- Large windows letting in warm, golden afternoon light (Rembrandt lighting style)
- Soft shadows creating depth and dimension
- Bookshelves in the background with design and technical books
- A comfortable, lived-in workspace that feels both professional and personal
- Perhaps a plant or two, adding life to the space

**The mood and feeling:**
- **Warm, inviting, and collaborative** — like someone you'd want to work with
- **Thoughtful and attentive** — showing someone who listens and considers carefully
- **Creative yet grounded** — balancing imagination with practical problem-solving
- **Relatable and human** — not abstract or intimidating, but approachable and real

**Artistic style:**
- **Classical portraiture with painterly realism** — Rembrandt-inspired lighting with soft, natural shadows
- **Rich, warm color palette** — deep browns, warm golds, soft creams, with perhaps a pop of color from the design materials
- **Focus on the face and hands** — showing both thoughtfulness and the act of creation
- **Depth and dimension** — the background should feel real but not distracting
- **Wallpaper-appropriate composition** — vertical or horizontal format that works well as a desktop background, with the subject positioned to feel present but not overwhelming

**The final image should evoke the feeling of: "This is who I talk to when I need to think through a problem, refine an idea, or build something beautiful. This is my creative partner."**

---

## Notes for Image Generation

- **Lighting**: Rembrandt triangle lighting on the face (one side well-lit, the other with a triangle of light on the cheek)
- **Expression**: Warm, thoughtful, slightly engaged — as if mid-conversation or about to share an insight
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, with the subject slightly off-center
- **Color temperature**: Warm (golden hour feel)
- **Detail level**: High enough to see the thoughtful expression and the symbolic objects clearly
- **Avoid**: Abstract or surreal elements, anything that feels "AI-generated" or uncanny, overly dramatic lighting, or anything that feels impersonal
